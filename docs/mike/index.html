Mike's Biography

Hi, my name is Michael Aguirre, a tattoo artist from Las Cruces, New Mexico, at Syncere Tattoo. I've been tattooing since 2021, specializing in high-contrast black and grey realism, horror tattoos, script, American Traditional, and religious/abstract tattoos. My goal is to live my dream by creating permanent, high-quality, meaningful art that lasts a lifetime and is appreciated by those who understand and respect the craft.

Tattoos have been a part of my life for a long time, and I've always been artistic. I've spent years drawing, painting, working with all kinds of different mediums, and taking art classes throughout my school years. I completed a two-year apprenticeship and have been tattooing professionally for a total of four years. I love tattooing because it's a beautiful form of self-expression—an exchange of energy, connection, and trust. It's such a unique craft, and I love creating meaningful pieces and bringing people's ideas to life.

I especially enjoy doing high-contrast black and grey realism, religious, and horror pieces. I love horror movies, religious artwork, and high-contrast art, all of which are reflected in my work. I prefer creating custom designs to ensure every tattoo is original and unique to each client.

Having good communication with my clients is very important to me. I believe it's essential to gather all the information and details needed to truly bring my clients' visions to life. I'm always transparent and honest because I respect every client who trusts me with their ideas. Designing something so meaningful shouldn't be rushed or thrown together. Here, we focus on creating a welcoming, safe, and comfortable environment for everyone.

I have four years of experience working in a professional tattoo shop and am CPR certified.

Outside of tattooing, I enjoy spending time with my family and my two cats. I'm a homebody, so I enjoy playing video games, cooking, watching movies, painting, and engraving mirrors.

I'm always accepting new clients, whether it's your very first tattoo or you're adding another piece to your canvas. I'd truly enjoy the privilege of helping bring your ideas to life through artwork that you'll wear and cherish forever.

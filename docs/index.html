DIANE BASLER-WHITE

Owner

Intricate fine line dot work, mandalas and geometric, floral, bold color, black & grey realism, animal portraits, and traditional.

Diane Basler-White owns Syncere Tattoo — named after her daughter — which she opened in 2007, the oldest studio in Las Cruces. A lifelong, self-taught artist, she works across many styles and has built a welcoming, inclusive shop focused on quality and pieces personal to each client.

FAQ

Call us at (575) 524-0190, email SyncereTattoobooking@gmail.com, or message us on Instagram @synceretattoo_nm. Share your idea, any reference images, preferred placement, and available dates so we can match you with the right artist and get you scheduled.
You must be at least 18 years of age. It is against New Mexico law to tattoo a minor, even with parental consent. Every client must present a valid state or federal photo ID — no exceptions.
Pricing depends on the artist, size, detail, and placement. We offer free consultations to give you an accurate estimate before you commit. Reach out with your idea and we'll walk you through options.
The best way is a consultation — in person at the studio or by email with reference photos and a description of size, placement, and detail. We're happy to answer questions and help you plan your piece.
Appointments are recommended. Call (575) 524-0190 to check same-day availability. We'll do our best to accommodate you when the schedule allows.

Surface healing typically takes two to three weeks. Full skin regeneration can take several months. We'll send you home with aftercare instructions tailored to your piece.
Follow the aftercare card your artist provides. Keep it clean, moisturize as directed, avoid sun and soaking until healed, and reach out if you have any concerns during healing.
We accept cash and major credit cards. Contact us if you have questions about payment before your appointment.
A deposit is required to hold your appointment. Deposits are applied to your final session and are non-refundable if you cancel without sufficient notice. Ask when you book for current policy details.
Eat a solid meal, drink water, and get good rest the night before. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours — it can increase bleeding and affect healing. Wear comfortable clothing that gives easy access to the area being tattooed.
